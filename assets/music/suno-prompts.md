# Suno prompts for the music takes

The style prompt and exclude list behind each take in `public/music/`, as
Suno shows them. Each file was then stretched onto the stage's beat grid
(95.08 BPM), after its own intro, and cut to loop from bar 8 of the song to
the end of its turnaround: after bar 48 for stage, impostor, bidding and
matchday, after bar 47 for booth and recess (`src/lib/music.ts`). The last
phrase of each loop is nudged by a few milliseconds so the beat at the loop's
end lands where it does at its start.

## stage-loop.mp3 (stage)

Style:

> Retro 16-bit video game menu groove meets 1970s game show lounge, unhurried steady 96 BPM, instrumental, FM synth bass holds a repetitive two-chord vamp beneath soft Rhodes comping and muted rhythm guitar, warm synth pad, low chiptune arpeggio texture, brushed drum-machine pattern and light shaker; sparse, spacious, mellow mix with flat energy and loop-friendly pad-and-bass intro and outro

Exclude: vocals, lead melody, melodic solo, instrumental solo, piano solo, guitar solo, synth lead, featured instrument, improvisation, virtuoso fills, saxophone, trumpet, harsh square wave lead, drum fills, crescendo, EDM
